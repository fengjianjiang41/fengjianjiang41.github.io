I’m a researcher in the [Lifelike Avatar & Agent Laboratory (LAVA)](https://lava.kaist.ac.kr) at KAIST’s Graduate School of Culture Technology, in Daejeon.

I trained as a physicist at Peking University, and I still approach graphics the way a physicist would: start from how the world actually behaves, then find a way to compute it fast enough to feel alive. My work sits where AI, computer graphics, and game production meet.

Outside the lab I play a lot of AAA games, which keeps me honest about what “feeling alive” means from the player’s side of the screen.
